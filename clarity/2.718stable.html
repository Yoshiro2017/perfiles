LaTeX Math Guide

info How to use: Type /math followed by any valid LaTeX expression — it renders automatically!

Examples

  • /math E = mc^2 → E = mc²
  • /math \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a} → Quadratic formula
  • /math \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} → Summation
  • /math \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} dx → Integral
  • /math \lim_{x \to \infty} (1 + \frac{1}{x})^x = e → Limit = e ≈ 2.718...
  • /math \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} → Matrix

Common LaTeX Symbols

  • Subscript: x_i · Superscript: x^2
  • Fractions: \frac{a}{b} · Square root: \sqrt{x}
  • Sum: \sum · Integral: \int · Limit: \lim
  • Greek letters: \alpha \beta \gamma \pi \theta

Known Bugs & FAQ

check_circle Bug Fixed ✅

Issue: Text sometimes invisible in preview — RESOLVED in v2.718 ✅

Why do commands disappear from preview?

Intentional feature! Commands like /serif, /monospace, /colorrgb are instructions — they apply to following text and do not appear in preview. Use backticks to display literally: `/serif`

How does /math work?

Everything after /math on that line is parsed as pure LaTeX and rendered automatically using KaTeX. Supports standard LaTeX syntax: fractions, roots, sums, integrals, matrices, Greek letters, etc.

Why v2.718?

Because e ≈ 2.71828... — Euler's number, the base of the natural logarithm! 📐 This version is math-focused by design!
