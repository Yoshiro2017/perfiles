About Telugu

Telugu is a major Dravidian language spoken in the Indian states of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. It uses a beautiful, looping abugida script — one of the most widely used writing systems in southern India.

About Thai Looped

Thai Looped is a distinctive variant of the Thai script where characters retain the traditional circular loops — historically the standard form. Noto Sans Thai Looped preserves this classic look alongside the loopless Noto Sans Thai and the more traditional Noto Serif Thai.
