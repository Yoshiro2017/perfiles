Known Bugs & FAQ

help Frequently Asked Questions

Why do commands disappear from the preview?

This is NOT a bug — it is an intentional feature!

Commands like /serif, /monospace, /colorrgb #ff0000 are instructions, not content. They are parsed and applied to the text that follows them — so they do not appear in the preview output. This keeps your preview clean while commands still work behind the scenes!

Can I type a command literally?

Wrap it in backticks: `/serif` → displays as code: `/serif`

Why v2.718 next?

Because e ≈ 2.71828... — the next release will focus primarily on mathematics: equation support, math symbols, and more! 📐

Headings look different from body text

Headings automatically use Display variants (Noto Sans Display / Noto Serif Display) in bold weight — these fonts are optimized for larger sizes and have a different design than body text fonts. Use /serif or /sansserif before a heading to switch its Display font.
