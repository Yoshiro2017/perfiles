⌨️ Why Does QWERTY Exist?

QWERTY wasn't made for speed — it was made to PREVENT JAMS. ⚙️

The Problem

Early typewriters jammed when typing fast. Common letters were too close together!

The Fix

Christopher Sholes spread common letters apart. Slower = fewer jams.

The Legacy

Everyone learned it. Now we're stuck with it — since 1873!
